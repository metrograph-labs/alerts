Indicators

Reading the indicators.

An indicator is a measure with a normal level, for example the grid frequency. Many dimensions out of their normal level at one time is a strong sign. The indicators are of the present, also when the map shows a past time.

The shadow score

Reading the shadow score.

The shadow score is the probability of an event times its deaths and its injuries, from public measures. It does not set a level yet. This panel lists each pair where the table gives the higher level to the incident with clearly less expected harm. The score is of the present.

Interests

Reading the interests.

An alert reaches a person through the edges of their interest graph. A default edge comes from a watched place, a kind of work or a service. An edge of the person replaces a default edge at the same pair, and the default stays visible here.
