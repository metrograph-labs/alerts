# Roadmap

This file is the plan of alerts. The Status page of the site reads it, and the tracker of the project mirrors it.

The goal of phase 1: the system does not miss a life-safety warning at a watched place. Each kind of input has two or more independent sources. The delivery repeats until the person acknowledges. A watchdog and an outside heartbeat watch the system.

The repo is public. It holds the general framework only. People, places, keys and the choices of the owner stay in a private configuration.

## Milestones

The target is the whole vision. The milestones give the order of the work.

- **v1: each life-safety warning reaches the person, from two copies.**
  - The official warnings for the watched places come from two sources for each kind.
  - They reach the person by push, then by a call. At night, only life-safety warnings wake the person.
  - A copy on one computer and a copy in the cloud send them. An outside heartbeat watches both copies.
  - The fault tests gate each change, and drills test each live path. A weather radio is the backup alarm with no network.
  - Items: AL-1 to AL-13, AL-15 to AL-18, AL-28 to AL-30, AL-32, AL-37, AL-39, AL-42 to AL-45, AL-47 to AL-50, AL-52, AL-54, AL-56 and AL-57.
- **v2: each class of signal, ranked by its real cost.**
  - The coverage grows to each class of signal in the world: more hazards, outages, news and radio.
  - The priority comes from the chance and the cost of each event. The relevance comes from the interest graph of each person.
  - The copies come from code, and the availability algebra gives their places. The alerts go to other people too.
  - Items: AL-35 first, then AL-14, AL-19, AL-21, AL-22, AL-25, AL-26, AL-31, AL-33, AL-34, AL-36, AL-38, AL-40, AL-41, AL-46, AL-51, AL-53, AL-55 and AL-59.
- **Later:** AL-20, AL-23, AL-24 and AL-27.
